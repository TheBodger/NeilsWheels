The long hot summer continues, with a special VIP trip to Longleat to see the Koalas, wander the opulence of the house and say hello to monkeys, lions and loads of noisy colourful birds.

A wondaful couple of days, with the main day starting in an almost empty park as we get in before the masses arrive. The Koalas are the treat of the day, watching them breakfasting from just arms length. The house is amazing, the grounds still sumptuous despite the ravages of the near drought conditions. And then we take a bus to see more African animals than, well any other safari park!
