 Back

A trip along the north coast of Devon and Cornwall. enjoying boats, stunning views, walks and lots of cream, clotted and frozen.

A longer trip with 4 sites. Starting in Devon in Exmoor in the banks of one of the river exe tributaries, with lovely peaceful walks, we then went further west to Watermouth via the stunningly pretty Coombe Martin (longest main street in the UK) 

Watermouth is near Ilfracombe, where we planned to view the sea cliffs and hopefully see dolphins (no luck). More importantly it has a beautiful harbour with some of the best creams teas available. We then moved onto Cornwall. 

On the cliffs between Tintagel and Boscastle, we at last had the view we always dreamed of; open the back door and all you can see is sea, hills and the coastal path!. Walking one way on day 1 and then the other day 2 we earned the gallons of ice-cream and cake consumed. There were plenty of birds to distract but all a bit too fast in the exhausting heat. 

Finally we headed back home, staying at a hotel/golf club to rest and recover with a final lovely lunch in Oakhampton. All in all a wonderful trip!
